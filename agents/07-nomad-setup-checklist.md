# Nomad Setup Checklist

Last updated: 2026-11-11. Goal: income and payouts that keep working while Dylan moves from South Africa to Tbilisi (4 Jan 2027) and beyond. Not tax or legal advice.

## Phase 1: Now to 4 Jan (still in South Africa)
1. Pick site platform and buy a domain. Domain and hosting must not depend on his location. Use a registrar account in his own name with 2FA.
2. Confirm Wise USD account details work (US routing and account number, in his own name).
3. Build the site shell: home, about (real, with his running background), disclosure page, privacy page, contact.
4. Write the first 5 to 10 articles from his real experience (Evidence Producer input needed: shoe wear logs, Strava mileage, photos).
5. Apply to Amazon Associates once the site has real content. Payout: Wise USD details. Tax form: answer truthfully for his residency at the time of signing.
6. Apply to 2 to 3 brand or retailer programs (Impact, Awin, CJ). Note each program's payout route and minimum.
7. Set up Google Search Console and basic analytics.
8. Ask a South African tax practitioner about ceasing SA tax residency, and what SARS expects on foreign income while he is still resident. Leaving does not automatically end SA tax residency (UNVERIFIED here, confirm with a professional).

## Phase 2: After arriving in Tbilisi
1. Update address, residency and tax form details in every affiliate program and in Wise.
2. Track days in Georgia from the first day. Georgian tax residency starts at 183 days in a 12-month period (REPORTED by secondary sources).
3. Decide on Georgian Individual Entrepreneur with Small Business Status (reported 1% on turnover up to 500,000 GEL). Reports say to register before reaching 183 days. A work-permit rule change on 1 March 2026 adds uncertainty about remote work from Georgia. Get a local accountant to confirm.
4. Keep clean records: monthly income per program, payout dates, Wise statements.

## Phase 3: Ongoing
- Monthly: reconcile program dashboards against Wise deposits.
- Re-check program terms (Amazon April 2026 changes) each quarter.
- Keep one backup payout route (Payoneer USD details or gift cards).
- Do not let any program account lapse its 180-day Amazon qualifying window.

## Source quality note
The Georgia tax details come from secondary nomad and legal-marketing sites, not Georgia's Revenue Service. Treat as leads, not facts.
