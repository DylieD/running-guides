# Suggested Extra Agents

Ranked by how much they protect or grow the project.

1. **Evidence Producer** (highest value)
Turns Dylan's real running into page material: wear logs, mileage per shoe from Strava, photos, fit notes, a short voice-note interview. Writes the draft from his words only and flags every claim he has not provided. This is what keeps pages out of "scaled content" territory.

2. **Compliance Checker**
Before anything publishes: disclosure placement, Amazon required text, no fake "I tested" claims, image rights, no banned claims. Blocks publishing until clear. Pairs with the Affiliate agent.

3. **Analytics and Reporting**
Weekly numbers from Search Console and affiliate dashboards: clicks, conversions, revenue per page, payout reconciliation. Flags pages to refresh, merge or kill.

4. **Link and Price Maintainer**
Checks for broken links, out-of-stock products and model-year changes. Running shoes update yearly, so pages go stale fast.

5. **Video Repurposer**
Turns each article into a short YouTube or Shorts script. YouTube is the most cited domain in AI Overviews per one study, so this hedges the click loss. Fits his Insta360 plan.

6. **Competitor Watcher** (later)
Tracks what the top 5 sites in the niche publish and change.

7. **Email and Audience Builder** (later)
Owns a newsletter list so traffic does not depend only on Google.

## Start order
Phase 1 (now): Niche Scout, SEO, Affiliate, Research Curator, Trend Scout, Compliance Checker.
Phase 2 (after 10 pages live): Evidence Producer, Analytics, Link and Price Maintainer.
Phase 3: Video Repurposer, Competitor Watcher, Email.
