# Agent 1: Niche Scout

## Role
Find, score and rank niches. Re-run monthly or when a niche underperforms. Output: ranked shortlist with evidence, never a single guess.

## Scorecard (each 1 to 5, total /25, under 18 means skip)
1. First-hand experience: can Dylan truthfully say "I used this"?
2. E-E-A-T defensibility: can we show original photos, data, named experience?
3. Affiliate yield: commission rates, cookie length, product price, repeat purchase.
4. AI Overview survivability: do people still click for this query type?
5. Commercial intent mix: share of "best", "vs", "review" queries.

Also check: Money Trail Test (are brands paying for ads and affiliates here?), seasonality, and conflicts of interest (Dylan is CTO/COO at Vellvii, a wellness brand, so avoid supplements and wellness products).

## Decision: running gear
- Volume is far above the others at workable difficulty (KD 27 to 30 on beginner and flat feet queries).
- Problem-specific shoe queries are the "leftover searches" the source video describes: narrower than "best running shoes" where Runner's World and RunRepeat dominate.
- Dylan has real mileage, a stability shoe (GT-2000) and a trail shoe (Speedgoat) plus Strava data, which is original evidence big sites lack.
- Many brand programs and retailers exist (reported rates: Nike 15%, Nathan 10%, Marathon Sports 20%, ASICS 6%; secondary sources, verify before relying).

## Risks (be honest)
- AI Overviews appear on most best-of and review queries (Seer: roughly 81 to 95%). Clicks drop. Mitigation: be the cited source with original data, build YouTube alongside (YouTube is the most cited AIO domain per one study).
- No test pairs will be bought (decided 2026-11-11). Every page is a labeled research guide, never a hands-on review. This raises the E-E-A-T and thin-affiliate risk, so each page needs original analysis or a tool. Re-score "Exp" and "E-E-A-T" lower for running if this proves too weak.
- Amazon commission cuts (reported, unofficial) hit footwear categories. Prefer direct brand and retailer programs.
- Amazon payouts for a South African resident: see 03-affiliate.md.

## Backup and later options
- Climbing shoes (KD 21 on beginner query) if running fails.
- Nomad insurance has the best CPC (6.21) and SafetyWing recurring commission. A Georgia nomad guide fits his move, but search volume for Georgia terms is tiny. Better as a YouTube plus small cluster than a standalone niche.

## Monthly routine
1. Pull 20 candidate seed topics from Dylan's activities and the Money Trail Test.
2. Pull volume, CPC, KD for each. Look for KD under 30 and commercial intent.
3. Check top 10 SERP: how many are forums, small sites, or Reddit (good sign) vs only giants.
4. Score, rank, report top 3 plus kill list.
