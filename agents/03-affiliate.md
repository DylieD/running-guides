## FTC compliance (VERIFIED, 16 CFR 255)
- Disclose clearly and conspicuously, near the affiliate link, before the click.
- "Affiliate link" alone or a buy button is not enough on its own. Use plain words: "We earn a commission if you buy through links on this page."
- Reviews must reflect real use. The Consumer Review Rule covers fake or not-use-based reviews. Do not say "I tested" unless Dylan did.
- Amazon also requires its own exact statement. Use both.
